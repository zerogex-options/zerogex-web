== WHAT'S NEW ==

* Your levels, on your own chart (https://zerogex.io/tradingview-indicator).
  Two indicators that put the ZeroGEX levels on the chart you already trade.
  The TradingView script is free and draws the Gamma Flip, Call Wall, Put
  Wall, and Max Pain on SPY, SPX, QQQ, NDX, ES, or NQ, with optional
  cross-alerts. The NinjaTrader 8 indicator
  (https://zerogex.io/ninjatrader-indicator), included with Pro, polls the
  ZeroGEX API and keeps the same levels current on its own — plus Pin
  Strike, the strikes carrying the most dealer gamma, and VWAP — so you
  never retype a number.

* ES and NQ (https://zerogex.io/es-gamma-levels). Futures are first-class
  now. ES and NQ have their own gamma levels and their own free pages, and
  they carry the same read as SPY, SPX, QQQ, and NDX everywhere else on the
  site. The levels come from the SPX and NDX options books and are converted
  to futures prices on the live basis, so what you read is quoted where you
  actually trade.

* Gamma Shift (https://zerogex.io/gamma-shift). What changed, not just where
  things stand. Gamma Shift shows the per-strike move in dealer gamma
  between two points in time, read three ways — and it separates out the
  part explained by contracts rolling off at expiration, so a shift caused
  by expiry doesn't read as fresh positioning.

* Pin Strike (https://zerogex.io/education/pin-strike-explained). A new
  dealer-positioning metric: the reachable 0DTE strike carrying the
  strongest restoring dealer gamma into expiration — the strike hedging
  pressure is most likely to hold price toward. It is its own read, not a
  rename of Max Pain or the walls, and it is now drawn across the gamma
  charts, the Live Bulletin positioning map, Daily Replay, and the levels
  pages.

* Three new metrics — Market Tide, Pair Comparison, and Volatility
  (https://zerogex.io/market-tide). Market Tide folds gamma and flow across
  the covered indices into a single bearish-to-bullish score. Pair
  Comparison (https://zerogex.io/pair-comparison) puts any two of SPY, QQQ,
  SPX, and NDX side by side on strike-aligned Net GEX ladders, with a replay
  scrubber to watch the levels migrate through a session. Volatility
  (https://zerogex.io/volatility) charts put/call IV skew alongside
  realized-versus-implied.
