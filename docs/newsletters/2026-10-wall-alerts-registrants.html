You signed up for ZeroGEX to see where the walls are. Now ZeroGEX can tell you when price gets there.

Wall alerts are new this week. Pick a symbol and a wall, and ZeroGEX tells you when price hits it, breaks through it, or bounces off it, in the app and by email.
 
  Built to stay quiet until it matters
▸ One alert per approach, not one per tick. Price chopping around a wall is a single alert. The wall re-arms only after price backs clearly away, and that distance scales with how fast the market is moving.
▸ Breaks that are real. A break needs several straight 1-minute closes beyond the wall, so a wick or a failed breakout does not count. A broken wall stays quiet for the rest of the day.
▸ Only the walls that matter to you. A strength slider ranks each wall against the same symbol’s walls over the last 30 days at the same time of day, so “Strong” means the same thing on SPY as on NDX. Set it to Strong and the small walls never ping you.
▸ Three noise filters. Responsive gives the earliest warning, Balanced is the recommended default, and Conservative sends the fewest alerts.
▸ Context in every alert. The price, how far it is from the wall, the wall’s strength, which test of the day this is, and, where our sample is large enough, how often walls like it have broken within the hour.
  What a day looks like

SPX spent much of Tuesday, October 13 around 7,800 and crossed it more than once. A replay of that session with the default settings (both walls, hits and breaks, Balanced) sends four alerts all day:

10:02 AM HITPut wall 7,800 SPX 7,802.82
11:30 AM BROKEPut wall 7,800 SPX 7,788.26
12:01 PM HITCall wall 7,800 SPX 7,796.64
3:11 PM BROKECall wall 7,800 SPX 7,809.30

The level went from support to resistance, and you would have heard about each turn once.
