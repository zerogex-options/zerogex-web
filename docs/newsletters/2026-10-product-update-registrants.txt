A quick reminder of what ZeroGEX does: it models where options dealers are
likely positioned, so you can read intraday price action the way market
makers hedge it. That means the gamma flip, the call and put walls, and the
levels price tends to pin, reject, or react around.

A good share of what follows is free, so you can look before deciding
anything.


== WHAT'S NEW ==

* The Gamma Terminal, free on a delay (https://zerogex.io/chart). Our
  flagship chart: price with the Gamma Flip, Call and Put Walls and Max Pain
  drawn on it, and beside it either two strike-aligned gamma ladders or net
  dealer gamma by price on the chart's own scale. Anyone can open it on a
  ~15-minute delay, ladders included. Members get it live, on every symbol.

* A free levels email every trading morning
  (https://zerogex.io/spx-gamma-levels). The gamma flip, call wall, put
  wall, max pain and net GEX for SPX, SPY, QQQ, NDX, ES and NQ, in your
  inbox before the open and formatted to paste into the free TradingView
  script. Sign up with just your email on any free levels page.

* Spread Monitor (https://zerogex.io/spread-monitor). Can you actually get
  filled? A new page showing how wide the option market is quoted, which
  side of the book is the expensive one, where the chain thins out, and how
  much of it has no bid at all. Each reading is ranked against the same
  symbol's own history at the same time of day, and when there is not enough
  history the page shows the measurement and withholds the verdict. Included
  with membership.

* 0DTE on the Daily Replay (https://zerogex.io/replay). Every replayed
  session now has an All expirations / 0DTE switch. On 0DTE, the Call Wall,
  Put Wall, Gamma Flip and Max Pain are rebuilt from that day's expiry
  alone, so you see the book that actually had to be hedged into the bell.
  The filter and the playhead live in the link, so sharing a 0DTE moment is
  a copy and paste. Free.

* Forecasts that keep score. The Intraday Cone
  (https://zerogex.io/forecast/cone), in beta, redraws every 15 minutes: a
  price band for the next 30 minutes to 2 hours on SPY, SPX, QQQ and NDX,
  with the odds that price stays inside it the whole way. A reliability
  panel scores those odds against a simple baseline, including where the
  cone is not beating it yet. The Track Record
  (https://zerogex.io/track-record) publishes every Daily Forecast we have
  ever graded, with the misses named and dated and the weaknesses we know
  about listed on the page. Both are free.

* Rebuilt for phones and tablets. The phone layout was rebuilt from the top
  bar down: a live quote that doubles as the symbol picker, a full-screen
  menu, and charts redrawn for a small screen, with drag to pan, pinch to
  zoom and press-and-hold for a crosshair. Tablets get the same treatment
  instead of a squeezed desktop page.


== WORTH READING ==

* How often do gamma walls actually break?
  (https://zerogex.io/education/how-often-do-gamma-walls-break) We measured
  737 wall tests across SPY, SPX, QQQ and NDX. S&P walls held about two
  times in three over the following hour, and Nasdaq walls were closer to a
  coin flip. None of the 19 features we tested, including which side of the
  gamma flip price was on, predicted which walls would break, so we rewrote
  our own site copy to match.
