== WHAT'S NEW ==

* One Gamma Terminal (https://zerogex.io/chart). The Gamma Chart and the
  Gamma Terminal are now a single page. A switch chooses what sits beside
  the tape: two strike-aligned gamma ladders, or the Strike Panel, which
  draws net dealer gamma on the chart's own price scale so each strike's bar
  sits level with that price on the candles. Nothing was dropped in the
  merge, old links still work, and the free delayed version now includes the
  ladders.

* Spread Monitor (https://zerogex.io/spread-monitor). Can you actually get
  filled? A new page showing how wide the option market is quoted, which
  side of the book is the expensive one, where the chain thins out, and how
  much of it has no bid at all. Each reading is ranked against the same
  symbol's own history at the same time of day, and when there is not enough
  history the page shows the measurement and withholds the verdict.

* 0DTE on the Daily Replay (https://zerogex.io/replay). Every replayed
  session now has an All expirations / 0DTE switch. On 0DTE, the Call Wall,
  Put Wall, Gamma Flip and Max Pain are rebuilt from that day's expiry
  alone, so you see the book that actually had to be hedged into the bell.
  The filter and the playhead live in the link, so sharing a 0DTE moment is
  a copy and paste.

* Hedging Flow and Gamma Weather (https://zerogex.io/hedging-flow). A new
  estimate of the dealer hedging pressure created by today's option trades,
  charted against price, with a 0DTE filter (beta). At the top, Gamma
  Weather gives a one-line read of conditions: Stable bid, Supported dip,
  Fragile rally, Unstable or Mixed. It changes only after a new state holds
  for two bars, and the five inputs behind it chart on one shared clock.
  Here is how it works
  (https://zerogex.io/education/hedging-flow-explained).

* Forecasts that keep score. The Intraday Cone
  (https://zerogex.io/forecast/cone), in beta, redraws every 15 minutes: a
  price band for the next 30 minutes to 2 hours on SPY, SPX, QQQ and NDX,
  with the odds that price stays inside it the whole way. A reliability
  panel scores those odds against a simple baseline, including where the
  cone is not beating it yet. The Track Record
  (https://zerogex.io/track-record) publishes every Daily Forecast we have
  ever graded, with the misses named and dated and the weaknesses we know
  about listed on the page. Both are free.

* My Dashboard, on every device (https://zerogex.io/my-dashboard). Your
  boards now live on your account rather than in one browser, so your layout
  and theme follow you to another computer or your phone. You can save, name
  and switch between several boards, and there are new widgets, including
  Momentum Divergence, Volume Spike, Position Within Range and the ORB
  Breakout Map.

* Rebuilt for phones and tablets. The phone layout was rebuilt from the top
  bar down: a live quote that doubles as the symbol picker, a full-screen
  menu, and charts redrawn for a small screen, with drag to pan, pinch to
  zoom and press-and-hold for a crosshair. Tablets get the same treatment
  instead of a squeezed desktop page.


== WORTH READING ==

* How often do gamma walls actually break?
  (https://zerogex.io/education/how-often-do-gamma-walls-break) We measured
  737 wall tests across SPY, SPX, QQQ and NDX over ten weeks. S&P walls held
  about two times in three over the following hour, while Nasdaq walls were
  closer to a coin flip. And it is the index that matters, not the product:
  SPY walls behave like SPX walls, and QQQ walls like NDX walls. Worth
  knowing before you size a trade off a wall.

* Forced Flow and the zero-flow level
  (https://zerogex.io/education/forced-flow-and-zero-flow-explained). How
  much stock the current option book would force hedgers to buy or sell into
  the 4 PM close as price, time and implied volatility move, and the price
  where that nets out to zero.


== IN CASE YOU MISSED IT ==

These shipped in late August and haven't been in a subscriber update yet:

* Your levels, on your own charts (https://zerogex.io/integrations). Free
  TradingView and thinkorswim scripts draw the Gamma Flip, Call Wall, Put
  Wall and Max Pain. The NinjaTrader 8 and Sierra Chart studies, included
  with Pro, keep those levels current from the API on their own, Pin Strike
  included.

* ES and NQ (https://zerogex.io/es-gamma-levels). Futures are first-class
  symbols, with their own free levels pages. The levels come from the SPX
  and NDX books, projected onto the futures price at fair value.

* Gamma Shift (https://zerogex.io/gamma-shift). The per-strike change in
  modeled dealer gamma between two points in time, with expiry roll-off
  separated out so it doesn't read as fresh positioning.

* Pin Strike (https://zerogex.io/education/pin-strike-explained). The
  reachable 0DTE strike with the strongest modeled restoring dealer gamma
  into expiration.

* Market Tide, Pair Comparison and Volatility
  (https://zerogex.io/market-tide). One bearish-to-bullish score across the
  indices, any two indices side by side on strike-aligned ladders, and
  put/call skew charted against realized versus implied volatility.


== WHAT'S COMING NEXT ==

A quick look at where ZeroGEX is headed. Roadmaps shift, often because of
something one of you flags, but this is the current direction:

-> Single stocks. We are extending coverage to individual stocks, most
   likely starting with the Magnificent Seven (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN,
   GOOGL, META and TSLA) and adding more as we scale. If there's a stock
   you'd want in that first wave, hit reply and tell me.

-> Years of history for backtesting. We are working to license historical
   options data going back several years, so a backtest can run across
   different market regimes instead of only the months we have recorded
   ourselves since this spring.

-> Global futures and indices. We are also looking into coverage for more
   futures and indices, such as the DAX, Euro Stoxx 50 and FTSE 100 in
   Europe, and the Nikkei 225, Hang Seng, Nifty 50 and KOSPI 200 in Asia.
   Licensing that data costs significantly more than what we use today, so
   before committing we want to gauge how much interest there is, and
   covering these markets may take a new, higher-priced plan. If you'd trade
   any of them with ZeroGEX, let me know which.

-> A more streamlined experience. An ongoing pass to simplify the platform:
   fewer clicks to the read that matters, and less on-screen noise.
