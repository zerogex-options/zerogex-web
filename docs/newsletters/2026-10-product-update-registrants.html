  What’s new
▸ The Gamma Terminal, free on a delay. ▸ Spread Monitor. Can you actually get filled? A new page showing how wide the option market is quoted, which side of the book is the expensive one, where the chain thins out, and how much of it has no bid at all. Each reading is ranked against the same symbol’s own history at the same time of day, and when there is not enough history the page shows the measurement and withholds the verdict. Included with membership.
▸ 0DTE on the Daily Replay. Every replayed session now has an All expirations / 0DTE switch. On 0DTE, the Call Wall, Put Wall, Gamma Flip and Max Pain are rebuilt from that day’s expiry alone, so you see the book that actually had to be hedged into the bell. The filter and the playhead live in the link, so sharing a 0DTE moment is a copy and paste. Free.

The Intraday Cone, in beta, redraws every 15 minutes: a price band for the next 30 minutes to 2 hours on SPY, SPX, QQQ and NDX, with the odds that price stays inside it the whole way. A reliability panel scores those odds against a simple baseline, including where the cone is not beating it yet. The Track Record publishes every Daily Forecast we have ever graded, with the misses named and dated and the weaknesses we know about listed on the page. Both are free.

Tablets get the same treatment instead of a squeezed desktop page.
 
  Worth reading
▸ How often do gamma walls actually break? We measured 737 wall tests across SPY, SPX, QQQ and NDX over ten weeks. S&P walls held about two times in three over the following hour, while Nasdaq walls were closer to a coin flip. And it is the index that matters, not the product: SPY walls behave like SPX walls, and QQQ walls like NDX walls. Worth knowing before you size a trade off a wall.
