# Referrals

*How the referral program works - your code, your link, what counts as a referral, and how earned months land on your bill.*

---

## What the referral program is

When the referral program is running, you get:

- A **referral code** - an 8-character code unique to you.
- A **referral link** - the easy-share version of your code (the code is the part after `ref=`).

Anyone who signs up through your link and goes on to a paid plan counts as a successful referral. Each successful referral earns you a free month, applied as credit toward your next bill.

## Where to find your link

Open [Account](/account). The **Refer a friend** section shows your link, with a **Copy link** button, and your current standings:

- **Signed up** - how many accounts have signed up through your link.
- **Subscribed** - how many of those went on to Basic or Pro.
- **Free months earned** - one for every referral that subscribed.
- **Months banked** - free months waiting for you to subscribe; they're applied automatically as account credit when you do (shown only when you have some).
- **Credit on your next bill** - the account credit that will be applied to your next invoice, when there is some.

## What counts as a successful referral

- The new user must sign up through your link. There's no field for typing a code at sign-up - the link is what tracks the referral, and it's remembered in their browser for 30 days, so they can look around first and sign up later.
- They must go on to an active paid subscription (on Basic monthly, that means the free trial ending and converting).
- Self-referrals are not allowed (system-detected).
- You don't need an active plan yourself. If you're not subscribed when a referral converts, the free month is banked until you are.

## How credit is applied

Each free month is added to your Stripe balance as credit worth one month of your plan (on an annual plan, a twelfth of the yearly price), and Stripe applies it automatically to your next invoice. Credits stack - two referrals in the same cycle mean two months of credit.

Credits are non-transferable and non-refundable in cash. They expire if your account is closed.

## Sharing your link

Two patterns we see:

- **Direct link** - easiest. Click **Copy link** on the Account page and paste it anywhere.
- **DM at a useful moment** - when someone in a trading community asks about gamma exposure, the referral link is also an answer.

## How the new user benefits

The new user gets the same standard terms as anyone else - the 7-day free trial on Basic monthly, or the 7-day money-back guarantee on every other plan (no extension via your code), and any active sitewide promo applies on top. On top of that, their first bill carries a referral bonus: **a month free** on a monthly or quarterly plan, or **10% off the first year** on annual. It applies to a first subscription only. You're credited separately, as described above.

## Privacy

The Account page shows you the email addresses of the people who signed up and subscribed through your link - hover the **Signed up** or **Subscribed** count to see them. Nothing else about their accounts is shared with you.

## Eligibility and program status

The program is switched on or off for the whole site. If you don't see a Refer a friend section on your Account page, the program isn't currently running.
