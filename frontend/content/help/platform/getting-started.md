# Your First 15 Minutes with ZeroGEX

*A guided tour from sign-up to your first real read of the dashboard.*

---

## What ZeroGEX is, in one sentence

ZeroGEX turns the options market into a real-time model of **dealer positioning** - where the gamma is, where it flips, and which signals are firing on top of it - so you can read intraday price action through the same lens a market maker would.

## What you should do in your first 15 minutes

1. **Create an account and pick a plan.** A free account lets you browse the public pages. Basic unlocks the live platform: the Main Dashboard, the Metrics pages, the Basic signals and the Strategy Tools. Pro adds the Advanced signals, Trade Bias, the Composite Score page, TradeWorkz™ bots and backtesting, and API access. See [Tiers, Access & What Unlocks Where](/help/platform/tiers-and-access).
2. **Open the Main Dashboard and start at Key Levels.** This is the page you'll open every morning. The strip across the top shows the Gamma Flip, Pin Strike, Call Wall, Put Wall and Max Pain, each with how far price is from it. The **Long γ / Short γ** chip next to the symbol tells you which side of the flip price is on. See [Reading the Dashboard](/help/platform/dashboard).
3. **Open Today's Read.** Just below the strip is a plain-English summary of the regime: whether dealer hedging is likely to dampen moves or amplify them. The dashboard starts in **Simple** view, which folds Today's Read and the longer sections. Click a section's title to open it, or switch to **Detailed** at the top right to open them all. Clicking the Read itself opens the full [Live Bulletin](/help/platform/live-bulletin).
4. **Read the Gamma Chart.** Live price with the flip, the walls and max pain drawn on it, plus the Gamma Rail beside the candles, which shows dealer gamma by price so the walls appear as bars. Switch between SPY, SPX, QQQ, NDX, ES and NQ with the symbol picker in the header. See [How to Read ZeroGEX Charts](/help/platform/reading-charts).
5. **Open one signal page.** On Basic, start with the [Basic Signal Dashboard](/help/platform/basic-signals-dashboard) - Tape Flow Bias is a good first page. On Pro, the [Composite Score](/help/platform/composite-score) page is the natural starting point. Watch how the score moves in real time and read the "How it's built" panel. See [How Signals Work End-to-End](/help/platform/signals-overview).
6. **Visit the Education Hub.** The [Gamma Exposure Explained](/education/gamma-exposure-explained) article is the central read for everything that powers the platform.

## The two questions every page answers

Every analytics page on ZeroGEX is built to answer two questions at once:

- **Where are we?** - the metric, the score, the level, the regime.
- **What does it mean right now?** - the trade-bias chip, the trigger state, the regime label, the directional cue.

If you can answer both from a single glance at a page, you're reading it correctly.

## Three things to internalize early

- **Signal scores run from -100 to +100.** Sign tells you direction; magnitude tells you conviction. See [Reading the Score Line](/help/platform/score-line). The **Composite MSI** on the dashboard is different: a 0-100 regime gauge. A high MSI doesn't mean bullish - it means trends are likely to follow through.
- **Advanced signals trigger; Basic signals warn early.** Each Advanced signal (Pro) shows *Triggered* or *Stand by* against an explicit threshold. The six Basic signals are advisory reads that carry no weight in the Composite MSI - when they disagree with it, treat that as an early warning of a regime shift.
- **Above the gamma flip vs. below it changes everything.** Above is "mean-revert and grind"; below is "trend and amplify." The Long γ / Short γ chip on the Key Levels strip tells you which side you're on - check it before reading anything else.
